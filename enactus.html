What it is

EnAccess Maps is an Enactus Melbourne project aimed at addressing gaps in accessibility infrastructure data. The platform helps people with physical disabilities find, report, and navigate accessible routes and facilities — information that's often fragmented, outdated, or missing from mainstream mapping products.

I joined as part of the software developer team in August 2026. The project is in active development across both a web platform and a companion mobile app.
