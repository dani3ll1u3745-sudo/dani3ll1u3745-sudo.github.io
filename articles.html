Covid-19 Data Exploration: SQL Analysis and a Tableau Dashboard

An end-to-end exploration of Our World in Data's Covid-19 dataset (210 countries and territories, January 2020 to April 2021), looking at how the virus affected different regions and how vaccination related to deaths among reported cases. I explored the deaths and vaccinations datasets with SQL queries, documented the workflow in a Jupyter Notebook, then built an interactive Tableau dashboard covering weekly new cases by continent, total cases and vaccinations by country, deaths by continent, and weekly new deaths against new vaccinations. By the end of April 2021 the data shows about 151 million cases and 3.2 million deaths worldwide, with Europe recording the most deaths (over 1 million). Vaccinations began in December 2020 and passed 1.1 billion doses by April, led by China, the United States and India.

Mapping Seattle's Airbnb Prices: A Tableau Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard exploring Seattle Airbnb listings and 2016 calendar pricing. Average nightly price rises steeply with size, from about $96 for a 1-bedroom to about $585 for a 6-bedroom, and varies widely by zip code, from roughly $65 in the cheapest areas to over $165 in the priciest. Weekly total calendar price climbs from about $1.2M in January to around $2M by spring, then stays fairly flat through the year.

Price, Stars and Families: What Drives Expedia Hotel Bookings?

A group data analysis of Expedia search data, narrowing about 158,000 records down to 3,141 confirmed bookings to see how hotel price, star rating, and whether guests travelled with children relate to each other, and whether those patterns change when Expedia or a competitor had better room availability. Using rule-of-thumb segmentation and k-means clustering in R, we found that customers treat price and rating as a package, that booking behaviour barely shifts with availability, and that a premium non-family segment spends about $84 more per booking than budget travellers.
