Price, Stars and Families: What Drives Expedia Hotel Bookings?

A group data analysis of Expedia search data, narrowing about 158,000 records down to 3,141 confirmed bookings to see how hotel price, star rating, and whether guests travelled with children relate to each other, and whether those patterns change when Expedia or a competitor had better room availability. Using rule-of-thumb segmentation and k-means clustering in R, we found that customers treat price and rating as a package, that booking behaviour barely shifts with availability, and that a premium non-family segment spends about $84 more per booking than budget travellers.
